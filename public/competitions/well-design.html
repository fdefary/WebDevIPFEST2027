Well Design Competition

Optimizing Well Design for Long-Term Energy Sustainability

About

Get to Know More

The Well Design Competition is a premier IPFEST event that challenges participants to create innovative oil and gas well designs that are both technically sound and aligned with sustainable energy practices. Participants are expected to integrate geological understanding, considering formation properties, structural complexities, and operational constraints into their designs. Core components of the competition include casing design, cementing design, and well trajectory planning, ensuring that each proposal reflects a balance between operational efficiency, safety, and long-term sustainability.
