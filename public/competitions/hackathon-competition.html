Hackathon Competition

Optimizing the Full Potential of the Oil and Gas Industry:
Integrating Digital Transformation to Enhance Sustainable Energy

About

Get to Know More

Hackathon Competition is an innovation-driven, team-based competition that invites undergraduate students from universities across Indonesia and abroad to solve real-world problems in the energy and oil & gas industry. Focused domains:

  • Reservoir & Production Optimization
  • Subsurface Resource Exploration
  • Asset Performance Analytics
  • Drilling & Geomechanics

The competition fosters collaboration between participants, industry experts, and mentors — bridging academic knowledge with real-world applications and contributing to the global energy transition.
