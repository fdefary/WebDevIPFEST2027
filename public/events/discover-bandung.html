Tour de Bandung

Discover the City's Soul — Bandung Awaits

About

Get to Know More

Tour de Bandung invites IPFEST 2027 delegates to discover the charm of Bandung through an exciting city tour. Traveling aboard a comfortable city bus, participants will visit the city’s iconic destinations, including the historic Museum Asia Afrika, which reflects the legacy of post-colonial solidarity, the vibrant boutiques and cozy cafes along Jalan Braga, and the timeless elegance of Hotel Patra. Blending history, culinary delights, photography, and meaningful moments, this journey offers a memorable opportunity for delegates to experience the unique spirit of Bandung together.

Where We Go

Places We Will Explore

Hotel Patra Bandung

Our elegant base of operations — a landmark of heritage hospitality in the heart of Bandung, offering a warm and sophisticated welcome to all IPFEST delegates.

Museum Asia Afrika

Steeped in history, this museum commemorates the 1955 Asian-African Conference — a defining moment for international diplomacy and solidarity among newly independent nations.

Jalan Braga

The crown jewel of Bandung's cultural scene — a charming boulevard lined with Dutch-colonial architecture, artisan boutiques, galleries, and iconic cafes.
