Tour de Bandung

Discover the City's Soul — Bandung Awaits

About

Get to Know More

Tour de Bandung takes IPFEST 2027 delegates on a captivating journey through the vibrant streets of Bandung. Aboard a comfortable city bus, participants will explore iconic landmarks — from the storied halls of Museum Asia Afrika, steeped in the history of post-colonial solidarity, to the stylish boutiques and charming cafes lining Jalan Braga, and the timeless grandeur of Hotel Patra. A perfect fusion of history, cuisine, photography, and unforgettable shared moments.

Where We Go

Places We Will Explore

Hotel Patra Bandung

Our elegant base of operations — a landmark of heritage hospitality in the heart of Bandung, offering a warm and sophisticated welcome to all IPFEST delegates.

Museum Asia Afrika

Steeped in history, this museum commemorates the 1955 Asian-African Conference — a defining moment for international diplomacy and solidarity among newly independent nations.

Jalan Braga

The crown jewel of Bandung's cultural scene — a charming boulevard lined with Dutch-colonial architecture, artisan boutiques, galleries, and iconic cafes.
