Tour de Bandung

About

Get to Know More

Bandung is a captivating event that takes the delegates through the vibrant streets of Bandung aboard the Bandung Tour On Bus. This tour will be a delightful journey as you visit renowned destinations such as Braga Street, celebrated for its stylish boutiques and inviting cafes, and the historic Asia Afrika Museum, steeped in rich narratives of independence. Each stop presents an opportunity to capture stunning photographs, savor exquisite local delicacies, and enlighten the delegates in the city's dynamic atmosphere. With its perfect fusion of cultural exploration and engaging experiences
