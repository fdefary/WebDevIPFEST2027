Gala Dinner

A Grand Evening of Celebration & Innovation

About

Get to Know More

The Gala Dinner is the grand closing event of IPFEST 2027 — a prestigious farewell night that unites delegates from universities across Indonesia and beyond. An elegant banquet atmosphere, live performances, and the highly anticipated announcement of competition winners make this evening unforgettable. It is more than a dinner; it is a celebration of excellence, collaboration, and the spirit of the energy industry's next generation.
