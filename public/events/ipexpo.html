IPEXPO

Showcasing Company Profiles, Competition Outputs, and Energy-Sector Innovation

About

Get to Know More

The IPEXPO Division is responsible for organizing exhibition activities that showcase company profiles, competition outputs, and innovation within the petroleum and energy sector. The division coordinates both the Company Expo and the Competition Expo as platforms for networking and knowledge dissemination.

IPEXPO

Grand Company Expo

Grand Company Expo is an exhibition event for energy, oil, and gas companies to provide an overview of information related to the respective companies by making interactions between students and industries possible. This event is open to all students and members of the public, particularly those with an interest in the Energy, also oil & gas industry. This event is aimed to enhance students' knowledge of the energy and oil & gas industries, with a focus on company management, human resources and development, technology, and especially The Job recruitment information to the company

IPEXPO

Competition Expo

This event is an exhibition designed to allow delegates to showcase their competition work to the general public interested in developments in the energy and oil and gas industries therefore the idea of project work which is developed by delegates could be learnt by public or students who are interested
